Meet Your Trainer

Award-winning. Certified. Built for Georgia’s professionals.

Nikoloz Melkadze is a PMP®-certified project manager, PMI-Certified Instructor, and L&D leader with 10+ years of experience across defense education, international institutions, and CRM consulting. He has managed portfolios of up to 15 concurrent projects, led teams of up to 50 staff, and coordinated international partnerships across 5 countries. He holds an LL.M cum laude from the University of Groningen and active credentials from PMI and the Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD).

As Deputy Director at the Defence Institutional Building School, he oversaw 35 programs per year across NATO/EU interoperability initiatives, coordinating 26 international partners and a 5M GEL institutional budget. He led a 5-year NATO accreditation project across 4 institutions and in 2025 was awarded the Medal for Exemplary Service by the Ministry of Defence of Georgia.

A lecturer at four Georgian universities since 2016 and a participant in the U.S. State Department’s IVLP program, Nikoloz brings international-standard rigor to every session. He has taught PMP and Agile/Scrum methodology to students who subsequently passed the certification exam, and now builds that same track for Georgian professionals through PMEC.
