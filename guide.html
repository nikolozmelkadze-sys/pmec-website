4. Detailed Curriculum: The 5-Module Syllabus

The PMEC curriculum is strictly aligned with the 2025+ PMI® Exam Content Outline, covering the full spectrum of modern project delivery.

  • Module 1: Creating a High-Performing Team: Defines the foundational requirements for team leadership, including ground rules, member empowerment, and conflict resolution.
  • Module 2: Starting the Project: Provides the technical framework for planning and managing project constraints including scope, budget, schedule, and quality through to closure.
  • Module 3: Doing the Work: Focuses on managing risk, engaging stakeholders, and executing tasks across predictive, agile, and hybrid methods.
  • Module 4: Keeping the Team on Track: Explores the human element of project management through leadership, emotional intelligence, and performance monitoring.
  • Module 5: Keeping the Business in Mind: Ensures project outcomes align with organizational culture, compliance requirements, and the delivery of business value.
